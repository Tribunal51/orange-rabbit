How to Play

Board

A pyramid of 21 circles arranged in 6 rows (1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6).

Turns

  • Players alternate, each placing a numbered token onto an empty circle in their own color.
  • Each player has tokens numbered 1 through 10, placed in order.
  • After 20 placements, exactly one circle remains empty.

The Black Hole

  • The remaining empty circle becomes a black hole.
  • It destroys every token in a circle directly touching it (the swallowed tokens score nothing).

Scoring

  • Each surviving token contributes its number to its owner's score.
  • The player with the higher score wins.
  • Strategy: keep your high-value tokens away from each other so a single black hole can't wipe them all out — and try to bait your opponent's high tokens into clusters.
