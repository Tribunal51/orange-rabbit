Fire rays of light into the box, watch where they come out, and work out where the hidden atoms are.

How to play

Atoms are hidden somewhere on an 8 × 8 box. You can't see inside. Click any numbered spot around the edge to fire a ray of light in, then see what happens to it.

How rays move

  • A ray goes straight until it gets near an atom.
  • If an atom is directly in its path, the ray is absorbed: a Hit.
  • If an atom sits one square ahead and one square to the side, the ray bends 90° away from it.
  • If atoms sit on both diagonals ahead, the ray turns all the way around and comes back out where it went in: a Reflection.
  • An atom on the edge reflects a ray fired into the entry spot right next to it, before the ray even gets in.
  • Otherwise the ray comes out somewhere. Its entry and exit get the same number.

Scoring (lower is better)

Hit1 point
Reflection1 point
Ray exits elsewhere2 points
Each wrong atom in a guess5 points

Turns

  • Click squares inside the box to mark where you think atoms are. Marking is free and does not use your turn.
  • Each turn you either fire one ray or make a guess. A guess checks all your marks at once, so you need exactly as many marks as there are atoms.
  • A wrong guess tells you how many marks were right and adds the penalty.

Modes

  • 1 Player: the CPU hides the atoms. Find them with the lowest score.
  • Versus: each player secretly hides atoms for the other. First to guess the other's atoms wins.
  • Co-op race: the CPU hides the atoms and both players share every ray result. First correct guess wins.
  • Turn by turn alternates actions. Simultaneous lets both players act whenever they like.
  • Online: pick the settings, send the link, and play on two devices. You only see your own marks.
