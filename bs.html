How to Play

How to Play

Objective

Sink all of your opponent's ships before they sink yours.

Setup

  • Choose grid size (5×5 to 20×20, default 10×10).
  • Configure your fleet — default is 6 ships of sizes 5, 4, 3, 3, 2, 2.
  • Place ships horizontally or vertical on the grid. Ships cannot overlap.

Game Modes

  • Standard: Fire 1 shot per turn.
  • Salvo: Fire N shots per turn, where N = your number of surviving ships.
  • Rambo: Choose between 3-shot or 5-shot games. Each turn you pick how many shots to fire (1 up to your current max). Shooting above the average triggers a cooldown — your next turn's max is reduced. For 5-shot games the average is 3; for 3-shot games the average is 2. Example (5-shot): fire 5 → next turn max 1; fire 4 → max 2; fire 3 → max 3.

Turn Styles

  • Turn-based: Players alternate turns. The host chooses who goes first.
  • Real-time: Both players select and lock in their shots simultaneously each turn. A draw is possible if both fleets are sunk in the same turn.

Winning

The first player to sink all of their opponent's ships wins. In real-time mode, if both players lose their last ships in the same turn, the game is a draw.
