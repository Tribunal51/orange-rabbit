How to Play

Setup

A grid of boxes is created (from 3×3 up to 9×9). Two players take turns. Choose between Classic or Dice and Boxes mode.

Classic Mode

  • On your turn, click any available line between two adjacent dots to draw it.
  • If your line completes the 4th side of a box, you claim that box and take another turn.
  • If no box is completed, the turn passes to the other player.

Dice and Boxes Mode

  • At the start of your turn, roll a dice (1–6).
  • You can draw that many lines in a row.
  • Completing a box does not grant an extra line — only the dice roll determines how many lines you play.
  • If you roll more than the number of available lines, your turn is forfeit and passes to the other player.
  • After all your moves are used, the turn passes to the other player who then rolls.

Winning

  • The game ends when all possible lines have been drawn.
  • The player who claimed the most boxes wins!
