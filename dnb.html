How to Play

Game Types

  • Dots and Boxes — claim territory by completing squares on a grid.
  • Dots and Triangles — claim territory by completing triangles. Choose a Grid layout (with diagonal lines) or a Hexagonal lattice.

Classic Mode

  • On your turn, click any available line between two adjacent dots to draw it.
  • If your line completes a territory (box or triangle), you claim it and take another turn.
  • If no territory is completed, the turn passes to the other player.

Modern Mode

  • On your turn, click a starting dot, then click an ending dot in a straight line from it.
  • All empty line segments between the two dots are drawn at once. The line can pass over already-drawn segments, but must cover at least one new segment.
  • After drawing, the turn passes to the other player.

Lines per Play

  • Single — one line per turn (default).
  • Random (Dice) — roll a dice (1–6) at the start of your turn. You draw that many lines in a row. If you roll more than the number of available lines, your turn is forfeit.

Turn Style

  • Turn Based — players take turns one at a time (default).
  • Parallel — both players choose their move(s) simultaneously. After locking in, moves are revealed together. If a territory is completed by both players' lines, the player who contributed more edges this round claims it. If both contributed equally, it becomes a deadzone (gray, belongs to neither). No extra turns for completing territories in Parallel mode.

Winning

  • The game ends when all territories have been claimed (or turned into deadzones).
  • The player who claimed the most territories wins!
