How to Play

Objective

Complete lines on your grid and cancel all five letters of BINGO before your opponent!

Setup

  • Choose grid size (5×5 to 10×10).
  • Your grid is filled with numbers 1 to N² in a random order.
  • Click two cells to swap them and customize your layout, then click Ready.

Modes

  • Classic: Players take turns calling a number. The host chooses who goes first.
  • Clash: Both players call a number simultaneously each round. Both numbers are crossed off.

Lines & BINGO Letters

  • When a number is called, it is crossed off on both players' grids.
  • When all numbers in a row, column, or diagonal are crossed off, that line is complete.
  • Each completed line earns you one letter cancellation.
  • Click the glowing BINGO letter on the left to cancel it — letters must be cancelled in order (B → I → N → G → O).

Winning

  • Once all 5 letters are cancelled, press the BINGO! button to win.
  • The first player to press it with all letters cancelled wins.
  • Penalty: Pressing BINGO before all letters are cancelled restores your last cancelled letter.
