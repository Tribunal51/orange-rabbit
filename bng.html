How to Play

Objective

Complete lines on your grid, cancel BINGO letters, and call BINGO!

Setup

  • 1–8 players. 1 player plays vs CPU.
  • Grid size 5×5 to 10×10. Place numbers by clicking empty cells.

Modes

  • Classic: Players take turns calling numbers.
  • Clash: All players call simultaneously.
  • Random Call: Computer calls numbers automatically (like Housie).

Crossing Off Numbers

  • Called numbers are automatically crossed off on your grid.
  • The most recently crossed number is highlighted so you can track changes.

Cancelling BINGO Letters

  • Click crossed-off cells to select a complete line (row, column, or diagonal).
  • When a valid line is selected, click any uncancelled BINGO letter to cancel it.
  • Each line can only be used once. You can cancel letters in any order.
  • Penalty: Clicking a letter without a valid line: −2 pts (Housie) or restores last letter.

Housie Scoring

  • First player to claim each pattern gets bonus points.
  • First H-Line: 5 · First V-Line: 5 · NE Diagonal: 5 · SE Diagonal: 5 · Center Grid: 9
  • BINGOs: 1st=25, 2nd=20, 3rd=15, 4th=10, 5th+=5 points.
  • Play multiple rounds — scores accumulate.

Winning

  • Cancel all 5 letters, press BINGO. For 3+ players, game continues until one player remains.
  • Last remaining player scores points equal to their cancelled letter count.
  • Premature BINGO restores your last cancelled letter.
