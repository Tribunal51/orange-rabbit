Classic - sum-of-neighbours conquest. Wipe out enemy regions; no attrition.
Age of Empires - two phases: Assign (alternate fixed placements, Go-style surround captures, pie rule) then Battle (day/night parallel turns; day = attack, defend, claim, or a whole-day double attack / double defend; night = adjust).

Fog of War (Age of Empires only)
When on, you only see opponent territories that touch your own. Otherwise the whole map is visible.
Troops per Placement (Age of Empires only)
0 = auto (10)
Assign phase: players alternate placing this many troops per turn onto an empty region. Auto = total troops / 10.
Actions per Day (Age of Empires only)
3 Actions: each day you spend 3 action points. A 2nd attack or 2nd defend costs 2 AP, and a double attack or double defend costs all 3. Legal days: attack/attack, defend/defend, attack/defend/claim, attack/claim/claim, defend/claim/claim, claim/claim/claim, double attack, double defend. Nights stay one Adjust.
Win Condition (Age of Empires only)
Dynamic: game ends after ⌊3N/4⌋ rounds (N = assigned territories), or earlier if all territories hit 1 troop. Most territories wins.
0 = auto (⌊regions/2⌋)
Random Deployment
When random, both players' troops are allocated randomly across the map and the game starts instantly (skips deployment phase).
