Fog of War (Age of Empires only)
When on, you only see opponent territories that touch your own. Otherwise the whole map is visible.
Win Condition (Age of Empires only)
Dynamic: game ends after ⌊3N/4⌋ rounds (N = assigned territories), or earlier if all territories hit 1 troop. Most territories wins.
0 = auto (⌊regions/2⌋)
Random Deployment
When random, both players' troops are allocated randomly across the map and the game starts instantly (skips deployment phase).
