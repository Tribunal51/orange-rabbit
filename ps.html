How to Play

The Field

A rectangular grid of intersections with a 2-square wide goal at each end. The ball starts at the center.

Goals

  • Player 1 attacks the bottom goal.
  • Player 2 attacks the top goal.

Moving

  • On your turn, draw a line from the ball to one of the 8 adjacent intersections (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal).
  • You can never reuse an edge that's already been drawn (yours, opponent's, or the pitch boundary).

Bounce Rule

  • If you land on an intersection that already has any line touching it (boundary, your trail, or opponent's trail), the ball bounces and you immediately move again.
  • A turn can chain multiple bounces until the ball lands on a fresh, untouched intersection — then play passes to the opponent.

Winning & Losing

  • Win: Land the ball inside the opponent's goal.
  • Own goal: If you put it into your own goal, you lose.
  • Trapped: If you have no legal moves on your turn (corner with all edges used, etc.), you lose.

Tip

Bouncing off your opponent's lines is a great way to chain long, surprise attacks — but watch out for traps near the corners!
